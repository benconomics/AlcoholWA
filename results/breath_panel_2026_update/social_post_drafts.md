# Social Post Drafts: Updated Washington DUI Threshold Results

## LinkedIn

New Washington DUI data: a result from my 2015 *American Economic Review* paper still holds.

In that paper, I used BAC thresholds in Washington to study whether the additional sanctions triggered at .08 and .15 reduce repeat drunk driving. The design compares otherwise similar cases just below and just above each legal cutoff.

The breath-test data now run through 2026; the four-year repeat-offense models use index tests through June 2022 so each case has complete follow-up.

- At .08, the estimated reduction is 2.58 percentage points in the original 1999-2008 window and 2.08 points in the updated 1999-2022 window.
- At .15, the estimates are 0.98 and 0.90 points lower, respectively.

The central pattern remains: crossing the thresholds associated with more severe sanctions predicts less subsequent drunk driving.

This is not an estimate of the effect of BAC itself. It is evidence about the consequences of the discrete sanctions that turn on at these legal cutoffs.
